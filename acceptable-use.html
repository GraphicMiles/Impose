Policy

Use Botocracy responsibly.

These rules protect people, systems, and the shared infrastructure behind Botocracy. They apply to prompts, provider connections, relay requests, and research tools.

Effective 14 September 2026

On this page

Authorized useHarm and illegalitySecurity abuseDeceptionPrivacy and rightsService limitsEnforcement

1. Use only what you are authorized to use

Use only accounts, credentials, systems, data, and endpoints that you own or have clear permission to access. Do not use exposed, stolen, shared, or improperly obtained API keys.

2. Do not facilitate harm or illegal activity

Do not use Botocracy to plan, encourage, or carry out illegal conduct, violence, abuse, exploitation, harassment, discrimination, or activity that creates a foreseeable risk of physical or serious financial harm.

3. Do not attack systems

  • No malware, credential theft, phishing, ransomware, destructive payloads, or botnet activity.
  • No unauthorized vulnerability exploitation, access-control bypass, or persistence.
  • No denial-of-service activity, abusive scanning, rate-limit evasion, or attempts to overload the relay.
  • No attempts to discover control keys, access other users' data, or reach private network targets through the relay.

Good-faith defensive security work must be authorized and scoped to systems you are permitted to test.

4. Do not deceive or impersonate

Do not use Botocracy for fraud, impersonation, fabricated evidence, deceptive mass messaging, spam, or manipulation that conceals material facts. Clearly disclose AI assistance where law, platform policy, or context requires it.

5. Respect privacy and intellectual property

Do not collect, expose, infer, or distribute personal or confidential information without a lawful basis and appropriate permission. Do not submit content that violates copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, contractual, or other rights.

6. Respect service limits

Do not evade quotas, rotate identities to avoid restrictions, scrape the service excessively, resell public relay capacity, or interfere with normal operation. Use your own relay for sustained or sensitive workloads.

7. Enforcement and reporting

Access may be limited or blocked when activity creates risk, violates these rules, or threatens service reliability. Serious abuse may be reported to affected providers or authorities where appropriate.
