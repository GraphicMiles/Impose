One-time setup

You are the seeded owner. Completing setup claims the owner role for this account and closes the bootstrap permanently: it cannot be replayed, from here or anywhere else. Afterwards, authority is only ever "which capabilities does this account hold".

  • You hold every capability, including adding and reducing other admins.
  • Your admin row cannot be removed or stripped. There is no lockout path by design.
  • Admins you add start with queue rights only, until you widen them.
