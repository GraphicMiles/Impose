About

A focused workspace for working with AI.

Impose brings conversations, model providers, research tools, and deliberate browser actions into one interface while keeping people in control of their data and decisions.

One workspace

Keep conversations, reusable prompts, memories, provider settings, and research results together.

Bring your provider

Connect supported AI providers or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint with credentials stored in your browser.

Actions stay deliberate

Browser actions require clear user intent, previews, and confirmation before consequential sends.

Local first by default

The core workspace stores chats, settings, provider credentials, saved prompts, and memories in browser storage on the device you use.

When you choose a model or tool, the information needed for that request goes to the selected provider, relay, or search service. The Data & Security page explains each path.

Provider choice without lock-in

Impose supports direct connections to multiple model providers and custom endpoints. Demo mode works without a provider.

Each third-party provider has its own availability, pricing, security, and data-use terms. You decide which services to connect.

Built for understandable control

Impose shows which model is active, asks before browser sends, supports local export and deletion, and offers optional PII redaction before a request leaves the browser.

Impose is an evolving product. Features described as optional or experimental may change as reliability and safety improve.
