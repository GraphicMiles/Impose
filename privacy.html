Privacy

Privacy, explained plainly.

This policy describes what the current Botocracy web application stores, what leaves your device, why it is used, and the controls available to you.

Effective 14 September 2026 · Contact rfarouq69@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Botocracy web application, its public website, and the optional Botocracy relay. It does not replace the privacy policies of an AI provider, search service, hosting provider, or custom endpoint you choose to use.

Current account status: the authentication interface is not connected to a server-side account database. It stores a local session marker for interface testing. Passwords and one-time codes entered in this build are not sent to Botocracy.

2. Information Botocracy handles

Information stored in your browser

The application uses browser local storage for chats, message content, provider configuration and API keys, relay configuration, app settings, saved prompts, memories, queued messages, and retention preferences. Pending authentication email and verification-flow state may be kept temporarily in session storage.

Information sent when you use a provider

When you send a model request, the selected provider may receive your prompt, relevant conversation history, system instructions, saved memories used for context, generation settings, and attachments supported by that model. If you connect directly, the request goes from your browser to that provider. If you enable relay delivery, it passes through the configured relay.

Search, images, and page reading

Search and image queries can be sent to the Botocracy relay and then to third-party search sources. Page reading sends the requested URL to the relay, which retrieves and extracts the page. Public relay access uses IP-based rate limiting. Short-lived in-memory caches may keep queries, URLs, and results for approximately two to five minutes.

Hosting and analytics

The public site loads Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare and the hosting platform may process technical request information such as IP address, browser or device type, page path, timestamps, and performance or security signals. Server access logs may include request paths and query strings.

3. How information is used

  • To save and restore your workspace on your device.
  • To send requests to services you explicitly select.
  • To provide web search, image search, page reading, and optional model proxying.
  • To enforce rate limits, detect failures, secure the relay, and maintain service reliability.
  • To understand aggregate site usage and performance.
  • To respond when you contact Botocracy.

Botocracy does not use the contents of locally stored chats to build advertising profiles. The connected providers you select may have different practices.

4. When information is shared

Information is disclosed only as needed for the features you choose, including to:

  • AI model providers and custom endpoints for generation and model discovery.
  • The configured relay operator when relay delivery, search, images, or page reading is used. A relay proxy can see request destinations, request bodies, and forwarded provider credentials.
  • Search and content sources including configured search engines, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, and websites selected for reading.
  • Infrastructure providers such as Render and Cloudflare for hosting, delivery, analytics, abuse prevention, and logs.
  • Authorities or affected parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights, investigate abuse, or address security threats.

Botocracy does not sell personal information.

5. Retention

Browser data remains until you delete it, apply a retention rule, clear site data, or remove the application. Pinned chats are excluded from automatic chat retention. Session storage normally clears when the browsing session ends.

The public relay uses short-lived in-memory result caches and in-memory IP rate-limit windows. Hosting, analytics, AI providers, and search services keep information under their own retention policies. Botocracy does not control those periods.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Use demo mode without connecting an AI provider.
  • Choose a direct provider connection or a relay you trust.
  • Enable optional PII redaction before prompts are sent.
  • Remove provider and relay credentials from Settings.
  • Export chats as JSON or create an AES-GCM encrypted backup.
  • Delete individual chats, all chats, saved memories, and browser site data.
  • Set an automatic chat-retention period.

To request help with information held outside your browser, email rfarouq69@gmail.com. Botocracy may need enough detail to identify the relevant request or log.

7. Security

Botocracy uses HTTPS on the hosted site, a restrictive content security policy, browser-native cryptography for encrypted exports, constant-time relay-key comparison, rate limits, and restrictions on relay fetch targets. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect provider keys, relay keys, backup passphrases, and devices with access to your browser profile.

8. Children

Botocracy is not directed to children under 13, and it should not be used by anyone who cannot legally agree to these terms in their location without a parent or guardian.

9. Policy changes

This policy may change as the product and its infrastructure evolve. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are published.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to rfarouq69@gmail.com.
